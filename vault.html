CommBiz Fast Payment steps

  1. Log on to CommBiz (commbiz.commbank.com.au) or the CommBank Business app
  2. Go to Payments → Fast Payment
  3. Select Pay from your business account
  4. Choose Pay to: BSB & account number (or PayID)
  5. Paste BSB, account, name, amount and description from the copy buttons above
  6. Review → Submit → authorise (dual auth if required)

Fast Payments travel on NPP — near real-time, 24/7. Dual authorisation may apply depending on your CommBiz settings.
